How does it work? +

Each tile has four sockets: wall, passage, or canal. The solver chooses the cell with the fewest options, picks a weighted tile, then propagates matching sockets to its neighbors. It backtracks if a choice causes a contradiction.

Rooms are pinned as multi-tile recipes before WFC fills their surroundings. A reserved route connects their doors. Paired stair landings link two independently solved floors. Press E beside doors or on stairs. Floor buttons preview a cutaway; they do not teleport the explorer.

# Dungeon sound library

All imported recordings are CC0-1.0. Audio files retain their original bytes.
`provenance.json` records each file's creator pack, source page, size, and SHA-256.
Only event-mapped sounds are fetched/decoded by the game; the remaining files are
available for future sound design. No third-party requests occur during play.

## RPG Sound Pack — artisticdude

- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-sound-pack
- Download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/rpg_sound_pack.zip
- License: CC0, as listed on the author's asset page (checked 2026-10-16).
- Weapon swings, spells, inventory sounds, creature voices, and creaky door.

## RPG Audio — Kenney

- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
- License: CC0; original `kenney-rpg/License.txt` included.
- Footsteps, doors, cloth, coins, books, and metal interactions.

## Interface Sounds — Kenney

- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
- License: CC0; original `kenney-interface/License.txt` included.
- Menu clicks, selection, confirmation, and rejection cues.

CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Playback applies per-event gain, modest pitch variation, peak normalization,
and distance/pan/occlusion controls. Original files are not modified.
Procedural ambience, music, dice and unmatched creature sounds remain synthesized.

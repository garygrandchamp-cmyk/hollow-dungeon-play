HOLLOW / FROZEN CAVERN

Original dark fantasy tiles • 4.8 m modules • 64 texels per metre

Orbit: drag to turn, wheel to zoom.
First-person: click the scene, WASD to walk, drag to look. Esc returns to orbit.

Canals: dark frozen water is not walkable. Cross on the broad supported stone-and-ice deck. No bridge is invented for a bend.

Socket order: north / east / south / west.0 = sealed rock, 1 = passage, 2 = canal. The support and blocker queries come from the actual geometry recipe.
